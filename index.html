Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Drop & Pop ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") handles user information.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information from users.

The Game may store limited gameplay-related data locally on the user's device, such as game progress, scores, coins, diamonds, settings, unlocked items, and similar in-game information.

Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads, including interstitial ads and rewarded ads.

Rewarded ads are optional and may be shown when the user chooses to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward, such as extra lives or other gameplay benefits.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information, such as IP address, device identifiers, advertising ID, app interactions, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and other technical data. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and improving ad performance.

Ad providers may use this information to show personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on the user's region, consent choices, device settings, and applicable laws.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and other applicable regions, the Game may ask users for consent before showing personalized ads or using certain advertising-related features.

Users may be able to manage advertising preferences through the consent message shown in the Game, device settings, or Google advertising settings.

Third-Party Services

The Game may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify users or devices. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Third-party services used by the Game may include:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Google Play Services
  • Unity services, if used in the Game

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Information Is Used

Information collected by third-party services may be used to provide app functionality, show ads, measure ad performance, analyze app usage, improve performance, prevent fraud, and maintain security.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Third-party providers may collect, process, and share data according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Children's Privacy

The Game is not specifically directed to children under the age determined by applicable local law unless explicitly stated in the store listing.

If the Game is made available to children or included in a child-directed category, we will take steps required by applicable laws and Google Play policies, including using appropriate ad settings and SDK configurations.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to use trusted services and protect the app environment, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
